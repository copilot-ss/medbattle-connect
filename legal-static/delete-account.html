Delete directly in the app

If you are signed in, open MedQuiz and go to Settings > Delete account.

Tap Delete account permanently and confirm once. Your account can then be deleted directly by you in the app.

Only if you cannot access the app

If you cannot access MedQuiz anymore, send your deletion request to medbattle1@gmail.com .

This email option is only the fallback path for cases where you cannot log in anymore. Please use the email address of your MedQuiz account whenever possible and include your username if available.

What we delete

  • Your account, profile information, and login access.
  • Your gameplay and progress data unless temporary retention is legally required.
  • Your optional uploaded avatar photos stored for your account.

Important notes

  • Store purchases may remain visible in your Google Play or App Store purchase history.
  • Account, profile, gameplay, and avatar data are usually deleted immediately and no later than within 30 days after your deletion request is completed.
  • Some billing or security records may need to be retained temporarily where legally required.
  • Account deletion is usually irreversible once completed.

Response time

We usually confirm incoming deletion requests within 72 hours and complete them as fast as possible.
