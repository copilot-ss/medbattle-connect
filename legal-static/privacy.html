CoppiCodes is responsible for data processing for MedQuiz. Fragen zum Datenschutz kannst du jederzeit an medbattle1@gmail.com senden.

What data we process

  • Account data such as email address, username, and login provider.
  • Profile and gameplay data such as scores, progress, achievements, and multiplayer lobby state.
  • Optional avatar photos if you upload a profile image.
  • Device, diagnostics, and redacted crash information needed for app stability.
  • Purchase and advertising related metadata where relevant.
  • Support messages that you send to us.

Why we process data

  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure.
  • To run quiz gameplay, matchmaking, leaderboards, and progress sync.
  • To fix errors, improve stability, and prevent abuse.
  • To handle purchases, rewarded ads, and support requests.

Services used

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Google or Discord for optional social login.
  • Google AdMob for ads.
  • Google Play or Apple for store purchases.

Security and data handling

  • We transmit personal data over encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to account data is limited to the systems and service providers needed to operate MedQuiz.
  • Client error and crash information is redacted before it is stored for diagnostics.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure on your devices.

Retention and deletion

  • Account, profile, gameplay, leaderboard, and avatar data: while your account is active. If you delete your account, we usually delete these data immediately and no later than within 30 days, unless legal or security reasons require temporary retention.
  • Guest mode data and app settings stored only on your device: until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • Redacted diagnostics and crash information: usually up to 90 days.
  • Support messages: until the request is resolved and then up to 24 months for follow-up.
  • Billing, tax, anti-fraud, or security records that we must keep by law: up to 10 years where required.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or export of your data where applicable. Du kannst ausserdem Einwilligungen widerrufen und dich bei einer Aufsichtsbehoerde beschweren.

We usually confirm incoming requests within 72 hours and handle them as fast as possible.
